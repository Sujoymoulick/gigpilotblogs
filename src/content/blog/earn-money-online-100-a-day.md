## 2. Monthly Retainer Services

Instead of constantly searching for new single projects, land clients who pay a fixed monthly retainer for continuous assistance.

```
Land 2 Retainer Clients ($1,500/mo each) = $3,000/month = ~$100/day
```

Services suitable for monthly retainers include:
- Social media content management
- Search engine optimization (SEO) maintenance
- Virtual assistance and calendar management
